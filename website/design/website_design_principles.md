VISUAL FIDELITY & CRAFT (The "FWA" Standard)

Vanguard is NOT a "Bootstrap" or "Template" looking site. It should look "Custom Crafted."

- Depth over Flatness: Use subtle gradients, inner shadows, and glassmorphism (frosted glass) to create layers.
- Texture: Use a subtle grain/noise overlay on the entire site to prevent "flat" digital looks.
- Motion as Life: Elements shouldn't just "appear." They should float, breathe, or glide. 
- High-End Typography: Letter spacing (kerning) and line height should be adjusted for a cinematic feel.
- Reactive Design: The background should feel alive and subtly responsive to the user’s presence (cursor movement or scroll).
