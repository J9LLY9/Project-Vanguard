# Mission

## Our Mission

Vanguard's mission is to make running local AI as simple and accessible as possible without sacrificing performance, flexibility, or user control.

We believe users should spend their time using AI—not configuring it.

By intelligently understanding both the user's hardware and the requirements of each model, Vanguard will automatically configure, optimize, and orchestrate the local AI experience while preserving advanced controls for those who want them.

---

## Who We Build For

Vanguard is built for developers, students, AI enthusiasts, researchers, and anyone who wants to run open-source AI models without becoming an expert in AI infrastructure.

---

## Our Commitments

Every engineering decision should move Vanguard toward one or more of these goals:

- Reduce unnecessary complexity.
- Maximize performance through intelligent optimization.
- Preserve user control whenever possible.
- Build upon—not replace—the open-source AI ecosystem.
- Prioritize long-term maintainability over short-term shortcuts.

---

## Success

Every release of Vanguard should make local AI easier to use than the release before it.

If users spend less time configuring AI and more time using it, Vanguard is succeeding.

Mission over features.

Users over technology.

Understanding over shortcuts.
